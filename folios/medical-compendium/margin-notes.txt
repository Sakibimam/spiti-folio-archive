MEDICAL COMPENDIUM - MARGIN NOTES
Transcription notes, three hands identified.

HAND A (primary scribe, 13th c.)
  Formal dbu-can script. Consistent line spacing. Responsible for the base
  text across folios 1r-84v. Ink is carbon black, well preserved.

HAND B (annotator, later 13th c. or early 14th c.)
  Cursive dbu-med. Appears only in the outer margins, keyed to the base text
  by small circular marks. Predominantly pharmacological substitutions -
  where the base text names a Himalayan species, Hand B frequently offers a
  locally available alternative.

HAND C (undated, likely 16th-17th c.)
  Heavier, less practised. Interlinear only. Mostly orthographic corrections
  and occasional numerals. Ink has browned considerably relative to A and B,
  suggesting a different preparation or a substantially later date.
