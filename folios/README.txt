SPITI VALLEY FOLIO ARCHIVE
==========================

Photographic record of manuscripts held in a stone vault above the Spiti
valley, Himachal Pradesh. Photographed over two winters by Tsering, page by
page, in gloves, by window light.

These files stand in for 11,000 high-resolution images. The structure is what
matters: whatever is in this directory at publish time becomes the archive,
and the feed address stays the same regardless.

Contents
  bhojpatra/            Birch-bark scrolls
  palm-leaf/            Palm-leaf folios
  medical-compendium/   Medical compendium with margin notes in three hands
  index.json            Generated inventory - file list, sizes, sha256 digests

index.json is written into the collection at publish time. It is what lets a
stranger enumerate the archive without any local state.
